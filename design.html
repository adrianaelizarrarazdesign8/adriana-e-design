Future Funds

Mobile experience
01 / 02
Brand Strategy / UX

Future Funds needed a digital presence that made personal finance feel approachable rather than intimidating — a site where users could explore investment, retirement, and financial planning services without feeling overwhelmed.

THE CHALLENGE.

Future Funds needed a digital presence that made personal finance feel approachable rather than intimidating — a site where users could explore investment, retirement, and financial planning services without feeling overwhelmed.

THE PROCESS.

FEATURED CASE STUDY / 02

Magic Shop

Book cover design
Publishing
Publishing / Visual Storytelling

The purpose of this project was to create a complete front cover, back cover, and spine for a fictional novel aimed at fantasy and young adult readers.

THE CHALLENGE.

The design needed to attract attention, communicate the fantasy genre, and create interest in the story while staying visually polished and publication-ready.

THE PROCESS.

I used illustration, typography, and layout to create a magical atmosphere that matched the novel’s theme. The project also included key publishing details such as the publisher logo, barcode, author information, and promotional text.

THE OUTCOME.

A complete wrap-around cover design that blends publication standards, branding, and visual storytelling into one cohesive, visually engaging product.

TOOLS.

ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR, ADOBE INDESIGN, PHOTOSHOP, TYPOGRAPHY, PUBLISHING DESIGN

Whinny & Mane

Social campaign
Brand storytelling
Campaign / Storytelling

The purpose of this design artifact was to create a social media advertisement for Whinny and Mane Horse Sanctuary, an organization dedicated to rescuing and protecting horses.

THE CHALLENGE.

Whinny & Mane needed a social advertisement that would raise awareness and encourage donations by creating an emotional connection to the rescue mission.

Branding / Visual Identity

BeatFest is a complete visual identity for a fictional music streaming app designed for adults and music listeners who enjoy discovering and streaming music.

THE CHALLENGE.

The brand needed to feel energetic, creative, and recognizable while connecting with a modern audience and the culture of music discovery.

THE PROCESS.

I developed a wave-inspired logo to represent sound and movement, then built a style guide with logo variations, typography, colors, spacing rules, and identity examples.

THE OUTCOME.

A cohesive branding system that demonstrates how BeatFest could remain consistent across digital platforms while combining creativity, organization, and professional visual identity design.
